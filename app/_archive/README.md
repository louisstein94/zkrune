# Archived routes

These pages are parked, not deleted. `_archive` is a Next.js private folder,
so nothing inside it is routed — the files stay in version control and in
typecheck, but the URLs no longer resolve.

They were moved here because the token and community surface (staking,
premium tiers, governance, whale chat, Zcash branding) pulls against the
compliance and eligibility positioning the product is moving toward, and
they were reachable from the homepage footer.

Nothing here has been decided. Two of them are still referenced by current
plans — the circuit marketplace is an active technical priority, and premium
access and governance are tied to token utility that may or may not continue.
Restore a page by moving its directory back up to `app/` and re-adding the
footer link in `app/page.tsx`.
